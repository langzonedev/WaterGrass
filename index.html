Take your time.

Drag anywhere in the garden to pivot the hose. Sweep left or right, or drag up for the far grass and down for the grass near you. The hose button starts or stops the water.

Grass deepens in colour as it drinks. Water one spot for a while to make a puddle, or aim at the bowl to fill the birdbath.

Use the arrow keys to pivot the hose and Space to toggle the water.
